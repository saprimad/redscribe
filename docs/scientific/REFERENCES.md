The eight ground truth (GT) numbers retain their original order. Methodology citations 1–4 correspond to register entries 8, 6, 1 and 7. Part I citations 1–5 correspond to entries 9–13. Each linked record was inspected for bibliographic identity and scope. Support below applies to the stated claim only. Local implementation and study-specific results require separate evidence.
