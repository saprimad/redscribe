# Research concept and manuscript plan

Working title: Development of a Kelantan Malay Speech Corpus and Reproducible Evaluation of Local Automatic Speech Recognition Models Using RedScribe.

Proposed contributions: reproducible local software framework; naturalistic interview corpus; empirical accuracy and computational efficiency; dialect-specific error analysis. Existing dialect corpora and benchmark frameworks preclude claims of being first without evidence.

## Draft objectives
1. Develop and verify an integrated local ASR benchmarking framework.
2. Evaluate selected local ASR pipelines on spontaneous Kelantan Malay speech.
3. Characterise linguistic transcription errors.
4. Assess accuracy–efficiency trade-offs.

## Proposed analysis (not approved protocol)
Corpus micro-averaged WER/CER; recording-level descriptive statistics and paired bootstrap confidence intervals; Friedman and Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon tests if assumptions and complete paired data permit; Kendall's W and paired effect sizes; failures reported separately; Pareto analysis; dialect error taxonomy. Distinguish pipeline comparisons from causal model comparisons. Preserve verbatim fillers. Semantic preservation, public comparator corpus and additional linguistic annotation remain HOLD. Avoid treating words/windows as independent recordings.

## Manuscript
Introduction and literature gap; framework architecture; software verification; corpus and ethics; experimental setup; accuracy and error analysis; resource efficiency; limitations and reproducibility. Candidate journals (unverified current scope/indexing/APC): Computer Speech & Language, IEEE Access, Speech Communication, JICT, Pertanika Journal of Science & Technology, SoftwareX (software-focused). No results or acceptance claims.
