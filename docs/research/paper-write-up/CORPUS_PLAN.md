# Proposed Kelantan Malay Interview Corpus (KMIC)

Working name only; name uniqueness not checked. Existing paired interview recordings and human-verified verbatim transcripts are a potential recording-level corpus. Exact duration, speaker count, lexical counts, dialect proportion and eligibility must be established locally. No timestamps in references; utterance-level evaluation requires validated alignment. Do not assume all speech is dialect.

## Preparation
Confirm permission for secondary ASR evaluation and corpus development; document reference provenance and transcription conventions; define inclusion/exclusion; prepare pseudonymous local manifest and integrity hashes; quantify duration, speech, vocabulary, language distribution and speaker characteristics; document overlaps, fillers and spelling variants; optional validated alignment and dialect lexicon; dataset datasheet and controlled access policy.

## Governance
Original consent, ethics scope, retention and secondary use must be reviewed before processing or sharing. Voice and text can identify individuals. No public participant audio, transcripts, individual metadata, hashes or derived examples without appropriate authorisation. Public software, synthetic examples and disclosure-reviewed aggregate documentation may be considered. Restricted access is a proposal, not permission. Public external corpus requires licence and training-overlap checks.
## Reference transcript provenance — documentary evidence

A signed external service-provider confirmation records that draft transcripts generated by RedScribe were checked against original interview audio and corrected word for word, retaining dialect, fillers and repetitions and marking unintelligible speech. The provider also confirms confidentiality and deletion of its copies of recordings, participant information, drafts and final transcripts. The original signed evidence is retained privately by the researcher; it must not be uploaded to this public repository. This confirmation supports documented human review, not a claim of perfect accuracy, independent multi-annotator agreement, or authorisation for secondary research or corpus release. As the references were corrected from ASR drafts, potential anchoring bias should be disclosed and considered in the evaluation protocol.
